Risk register

Things that could break the timeline. Catching them now is cheaper than catching them at Day 60.

Email deliverability
High
Resend setup + domain auth has a 1–2 week warm-up period. Start Week 1, not Week 3. Mitigation: Begin DNS setup + warm-up sends on Apr 25.
Voice quality drift
High
The differentiator is "AI in your voice" — if drafts still sound generic after onboarding, churn hits. Mitigation: Ship voice training (60-day) behind a private flag Day 30 and tune with your own data first.
Scope creep from first users
Medium
First 3 coaches will each ask for different features. Saying yes to all of them delays everyone. Mitigation: Requests only get built if 3+ coaches ask independently.
Chief of Staff onboarding lag
Medium
Interviews Fri Apr 24 → likely hire Week 1 → 2–4 weeks to ramp. Don't build plans assuming full leverage before Day 45. Mitigation: CoS owns onboarding docs + support, not core build.
IG DM API access
Low
Meta's Instagram Graph API requires business verification + 1–2 week review. Only blocks the Day 90 multi-channel work. Mitigation: Start Meta app review Day 60.
Agency revenue pulling focus
Low
Agency is runway — and can quietly consume 100% of the week. Mitigation: Block 3 build days/week (e.g. Tue/Wed/Thu mornings) on calendar, non-negotiable.
