The Embodied Marketing Canon

What actually works when coaches market — and why most of it is bone-old, not new.

A field guide to the books, papers, voices and operating laws behind embodied / resonance marketing — the kind of marketing that travels through a coach's nervous system before it touches an ad account.

Curated for the Brand OS thesis: clarity → voice → pillars → content. Everything below is filtered for two questions — does it compound for 10 years? and does it survive an embodied coach actually saying it out loud?

01 · The eternal stack

What has worked forever — and will keep working long after the algorithm changes.

Every "new" marketing tactic on TikTok is a re-skin of something Hopkins or Schwartz wrote about 60–100 years ago. If you only read 16 marketing books in your life, read these. They are the substrate.

1923 · DIRECT RESPONSE

Scientific Advertising

Claude Hopkins

The original. Be specific, not clever. Measure everything. Samples beat claims. "We sell pianos by the millions" loses to "496 in March, here are their addresses."

Gold: Specificity converts. Vagueness dies.
1936 · INFLUENCE

How to Win Friends and Influence People

Dale Carnegie

Predates "marketing" but is the marketing book. Be interested, not interesting. Use a person's name. The deepest principle in coach marketing: they're the hero of their own story, not you.

Gold: Curiosity is the most underused conversion lever.
1966 · COPYWRITING

Breakthrough Advertising

Eugene Schwartz

The two ideas that explain why most coach offers fail: awareness levels (cold → most aware) and market sophistication (claim → bigger claim → mechanism → identity). When your market is saturated, you have to change the conversation, not amplify it.

Gold: Match your message to the stage of awareness — or get ignored.
1984 · PSYCHOLOGY

Influence

Robert Cialdini

The six (now seven) principles: reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, unity. Every funnel, ad, and DM is a remix of these. Used badly = cringe. Used embodied = inevitable.

Gold: People say yes for reasons that are predictable, not rational.
1983 · CRAFT

Ogilvy on Advertising

David Ogilvy

"On average, five times as many people read the headline as the body copy." Research, headlines, long copy beats short copy when the offer is high-ticket. Still true for $12K coaching.

Gold: The headline is 80% of the work.
1984 · POSITIONING

Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind

Al Ries & Jack Trout

You don't win by being better. You win by owning a slot in someone's head no one else owns. For a coach: not "embodiment" — too crowded. The slot is more like "the embodied AI marketing coach" — a fork no one else stands at.

Gold: Distinct > better.
2007 · MEMORY

Made to Stick

Chip & Dan Heath

SUCCES — Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story. The reason a frame like "Build Your Brand" travels and "marketing services" doesn't. Stickiness is engineerable.

Gold: Concrete > abstract. Always.
2010 · ACADEMIC

How Brands Grow

Byron Sharp (Ehrenberg-Bass)

The most under-read book in coaching. Brands grow via mental availability (you come to mind) and physical availability (you're easy to buy) — not loyalty. Most coaches over-invest in retention and under-invest in being remembered.

Gold: Reach > targeting. Distinctive assets > differentiation.
2013 · ACADEMIC

The Long and the Short of It

Les Binet & Peter Field

The 60/40 rule: 60% of marketing investment should be brand-building (slow, emotional, broad), 40% activation (sales, conversion). Most coaches do 0/100 and wonder why every month is a hustle.

Gold: Brand is the long game. Without it, every month is a panic.
2013 · SHARING

Contagious

Jonah Berger

STEPPS — Social currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical value, Stories. The science of why things spread. The "embodied AI coach" frame works because it triggers identity ("I'm a serious coach"), not just utility.

Gold: People share what makes them look good to themselves.
2016 · PSYCHOLOGY

Pre-Suasion

Robert Cialdini

The frame you set before the message decides whether the message lands. For coaches: the energy of the call, the ambience of the page, the breath in the reel — all pre-suasion. Sales doesn't start when you ask for the sale.

Gold: Persuasion is 90% about the moment before you speak.
2017 · NARRATIVE

Building a StoryBrand

Donald Miller

The customer is the hero. You are the guide. Stop being the protagonist of your own marketing. This single reframe fixes 80% of coach websites overnight.

Gold: Hero = client. Guide = you. Plan = your method.
2017 · FUNNELS

Expert Secrets

Russell Brunson

Crass packaging, deep mechanics. The opportunity switch, the attractive character, the cause. Strip the bro-aesthetic, the bones are real: people don't buy more of the same — they buy a new opportunity.

Gold: Sell the next vehicle, not the same hill with more reps.
2018 · PERMISSION

This Is Marketing

Seth Godin

"The smallest viable audience." Marketing for people who want to be marketed to. The death of interruption, the birth of trust at scale. Every embodied coach's manifesto.

Gold: Find 100 true believers, not 100,000 vague nods.
2019 · POSITIONING

Obviously Awesome

April Dunford

The most usable positioning framework alive. Five components: alternatives, attributes, value, customers, market category. Best 90-minute exercise a coach can do before launching anything.

Gold: Your category is a choice, not a default.
2021 · OFFERS

$100M Offers

Alex Hormozi

Aggressive aesthetics, useful math. The value equation: Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice). Most coaching offers fail in the denominator, not the numerator.

Gold: Shrink time-to-result, shrink effort — price holds.
02 · The resonance lens

Books for the embodied side — where marketing meets nervous system.

These aren't strictly marketing books. They're what makes a coach's marketing resonate. Most marketing books teach the outer game; these teach the inner state the outer game is leaking from.

2017 · HUMANS

Marketing Rebellion

Mark Schaefer

"Two-thirds of marketing happens without you." Customers run the brand now. The job is to build something so embodied that they carry it. The closest mainstream book to the resonance thesis.

Gold: The most human company wins.
2018 · STORY

Story Driven

Bernadette Jiwa

Brand <- backstory + values + purpose + vision + focus + strategy. A worksheet, basically — but the right worksheet. Where StoryBrand puts the customer at center, this puts your why at center.

Gold: Your story is the strategy. Skip it and everything else wobbles.
2014 · POSITIONING

Key Person of Influence

Daniel Priestley

The 5 Ps: Pitch, Publish, Product, Profile, Partnership. The clearest path from "coach who's good" to "coach who's known." Reads as a checklist for the next 24 months.

Gold: Authority is built in this exact order. Skipping = lost years.
2015 · DEMAND

Oversubscribed

Daniel Priestley

Build demand before you launch. Capacity < demand = price holds, energy holds, you're not desperate. Embodied marketing requires the nervous system of a man who is not waiting on the next sale.

Gold: Scarcity manufactured = sleazy. Scarcity real = magnetic.
2018 · POV

Be Less Zombie / 1000 True Fans

Elliot Kay / Kevin Kelly (essay)

Kelly's "1000 True Fans" essay (2008) is still the cleanest math in creator economics. 1,000 × $100/yr = $100K. For a coach: 100 × $1,200 = same. Niche down, deepen, don't widen.

Gold: You don't need an audience. You need a depth.
2019 · CRAFT

Almanack of Naval Ravikant

Eric Jorgenson (curator)

Specific knowledge, accountability, leverage (code, media, capital). The clearest articulation of why a coach with a strong POV + media leverage out-earns a coach with a bigger team.

Gold: Specific knowledge can't be trained, only attracted.
1997 · EMBODIED

The Way of the Superior Man

David Deida

Not a marketing book. The marketing book for embodied men. Presence, purpose, edge — what every "high-ticket coach" thumbnail is trying to imitate and failing because it's cosplay, not embodied.

Gold: Energy travels through copy. You can feel the man behind the page.
1990 · DEEP

Iron John

Robert Bly

The mythic floor of men's work. If you market to men in transition without having read this, you're working off second-hand symbols. Marketing that lands with embodied men carries this lineage whether they've read it or not.

Gold: Symbols older than the algorithm always outperform clickbait.
2008 · SOUL

Soulcraft

Bill Plotkin

Descent, eldership, soul initiation. Replaces the bro "level up" frame with something older and more honest. Resonance marketing for second-half-of-life men starts here.

Gold: Initiation, not optimization, is what most clients are actually buying.
2011 · ATTENTION

Thinking, Fast and Slow

Daniel Kahneman

System 1 (fast, emotional, embodied) is what scrolls. System 2 (slow, deliberate) is what signs the contract. Most coaches design for System 2 and wonder why no one stops scrolling.

Gold: Stop the body first. The mind follows.
2018 · SOLO

Company of One

Paul Jarvis

The anti-scaling book. "Better, not bigger." Permission for a coach to choose depth, margin, and life over headcount. The economic foundation for an embodied practice.

Gold: Capacity is a choice, not a limit.
2021 · ESSAY

The 2-Hour Writer / One-Person Business

Dan Koe (essays)

The contemporary translation of Naval for solopreneurs. Writing as the leverage. Audience as compounding asset. Especially useful for coaches who write more than they speak.

Gold: Daily writing is the cheapest distinct asset on earth.

04 · The shift

What's quietly dying. What's rising right now.

Most "what's new" lists are noise. These two columns are what we've watched move between 2022 and mid-2026 across coach marketing specifically.

✕ Died / dying

The tactics that used to work, and don't anymore.

  • Cold mass DM automationPlatforms throttle it, audiences spot it, deliverability tanks. The 2018–2022 LinkedIn DM playbook is a deliverability liability now.
  • Engagement-bait posts"Comment GO for my free PDF" works once, then trains the algorithm to suppress you. Reach-quality fell off a cliff.
  • Generic "value bomb" posts"5 tips for…" without a POV. Indistinguishable from ChatGPT. Reach collapsed mid-2024.
  • Loud bro-marketing for thoughtful coachesLambo thumbnails, "I made $1M in 30 days." Embodied audiences (the high-paying ones) read it as a red flag now.
  • SEO content farmsGoogle's Helpful Content Update + AI Overviews shredded thin content. Volume-only SEO is dead for solo coaches.
  • Massive launch sequences14-email "huge open cart" launches feel dated and exhausting. Evergreen + soft cohorts converting better.
  • Generic AI slopMass GPT-drafted posts published raw. Audiences clock it in 2 seconds. Trust burn is permanent.
  • Interruption ads at scaleTop-of-funnel cold ads to broad audiences = brutal CAC for coaches. Brand-led inbound is the cheaper math now.
↑ Rising / working now

What we've watched outperform across 2024–2026.

  • POV-led daily contentOne voice, one angle, daily. Justin Welsh, Dan Koe, Charlie Morningstar template. The most reliable audience builder right now.
  • Long-form podcast as trust engineOne 60-min conversation > 30 posts for closing $5K+ offers. The audio + clips loop is the cheapest trust at scale.
  • Niche newsletters (Beehiiv, Substack)Owned audience, ad-revenue optional, deliverability you control. The Signal's stack.
  • IG / LinkedIn carouselsStill the highest-ROI single asset format for B2B-ish coaching. Educational, screenshot-able, evergreen.
  • AI-assisted, human-finished contentAI drafts the scaffold, human delivers the embodied edit. The new content velocity ceiling.
  • AEO / GEO / LLMO optimizationBeing citeable by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude. New search surface, mostly uncontested by coaches.
  • Small high-trust cohorts$2K–$5K, 8–10 people, hands-on. Beating both the $97 course and the $25K mastermind on close rate + delivery.
  • Embodied / somatic angleNervous-system-aware marketing language. "Felt sense" beating "mindset." Quiet wave, real signal.
  • Voice-first creationWispr Flow, Granola, voice → text → polish. Closes the gap between embodied thinking and shippable content.
  • Communities as the moatDiscord, Circle, Mighty Networks. The retention layer underneath every modern coaching business.
05 · The 80/20

Five activities produce ~80% of coach revenue. Almost everything else is theatre.

Across the coach businesses we've studied — sub-$10K through $1M+ solo — the same handful of activities show up disproportionately. Estimated weighting based on observed pipeline contribution, not from a study.

Where revenue actually comes from

Estimated share of total revenue contribution for a solo coach in growth mode.

LAW 01 · 30%
Consistent POV content on one channel
Daily-ish, distinctive voice, one platform deep. The compounding asset.
LAW 02 · 20%
An owned audience (email list)
Platforms are rented land. The list is yours. Almost all $50K+ launches close from email.
LAW 03 · 15%
Trust accelerators (case studies, testimonials)
Specific, named, with numbers. Worth 10× of generic praise.
LAW 04 · 15%
Direct 1:1 conversation
DMs, voice notes, sales calls. The conversion happens in dialogue, not content.
LAW 05 · 10%
One signature asset (book / framework / quiz)
The thing they reference when they recommend you. Brand OS, ELEVATE Method, Build Your Brand are all candidates.
EVERYTHING ELSE · 10%
Ads, automations, partnerships, SEO experiments
Worth running, never worth prioritizing over the five above.
06 · Easy vs hard

The easy stuff doesn't compound. The hard stuff is mostly nervous system.

Almost every coach can do the left column tomorrow. Almost none can do the right column for 24 months. The right column is the only column that builds equity.

Easy / low compound
Anyone can do these
  • Posting "5 tips for…"Trains nothing distinctive in the algorithm or the audience.
  • Buying ads to a cold offerWithout trust scaffolding, the math rarely closes for $2K+ offers.
  • Hiring an agency to "do marketing"You outsource your voice. The audience feels it.
  • Repurposing across 6 platformsSix half-presences ≠ one strong one.
  • Letting AI draft and publishing rawSaves 2 hours, costs 6 months of brand trust.
  • Chasing trends weeklyFollowers ≠ buyers. Trend-chasing trains the wrong audience.
  • Launching a new offer every quarterYou never compound; you keep restarting.
Hard / high compound
Almost no one sustains these
  • Original thinking, every week, in publicThe single highest-leverage marketing act. Demands taste, conviction, and reps.
  • Showing up for 24+ months on one channelThe compounding window starts at month 18 for almost everyone.
  • A distinct voice that's actually yoursRequires a year of writing the wrong way to find the right way.
  • Embodied presence on calls and contentYou can't fake it past a discovery call. Nervous-system work IS marketing work.
  • Holding your price under pressureDiscounting once teaches the market your real price.
  • Saying no to the wrong clientEvery wrong-fit client costs a reference, a testimonial, and 6 months of brand drift.
  • Sitting with not-knowingThe strategic patience to keep building before traction is visible.
07 · AI automation map

What you can hand to AI, what AI can help with, what stays in your body.

The mistake is at the edges: thinking AI does nothing useful (slow), or thinking AI can produce embodied content (loses trust). The truth is a three-zone map.

High — delegate fully

AI runs it. You review & ship.
  • RepurposingBlog → carousel → reel script → email → tweet thread. Same idea, six surfaces.
  • SEO & AEO researchKeyword clusters, competitor maps, schema markup, AI-search optimization.
  • Outline drafts & structureFirst-draft scaffolds for posts, scripts, sales pages.
  • Image generationHero images, post graphics, mockups. Brand-locked templates.
  • Transcription & summarizationGranola, Wispr, otter. Calls → notes → action items.
  • Lead scoring & CRM enrichmentApollo, Clay. Enrichment runs without you.
  • Scheduling & calendar logisticsBooking, reminders, follow-ups.
  • Newsletter formattingReference-design templates → fill → preview → send. The Signal's pipeline.
  • Comment / DM triageSort, label, prioritize. AI never replies as you, but it can route.

Medium — co-pilot

AI assists. You shape the soul.
  • Hook & headline iteration10 options, you pick the one with edge.
  • Email sequence draftsAI does scaffolding; you rewrite for voice + nervous system.
  • Research synthesisThis document is an example. Source → structure → you make it land.
  • Audience researchPulling DMs, reviews, comments into themes. You decide what's signal.
  • Brand voice enforcementAI grades a draft against your voice spec. You make the call.
  • Funnel diagnosticsAI maps drop-offs. You decide the intervention.
  • Coaching content prepFrameworks, exercises, prompts to use with clients.
  • Carousel + reel scriptsAI drafts; you re-record and re-cut for embodied feel.

Low — must stay yours

Outsource these and the brand dies.
  • Original POVYour contrarian take. The thing only you can say with conviction.
  • Embodied presence on callsVoice tone, breath, eye contact, silence. Not delegable.
  • Story selectionWhich of your stories you tell, and which you don't. This is taste.
  • Trust signalsReal cases, real numbers, real names. Fabricated = brand suicide.
  • 1:1 high-trust DMsThe 30 messages a month that close $5K+. Type them yourself.
  • Pricing & the energy of "no"Holding the price. Walking away. Felt, not scripted.
  • The missionWhy you do this. Drift here = drift everywhere.
08 · The operating playbook

How a resonance-led coach actually runs this — in 6 moves.

Distilled from the canon above and filtered through the Brand OS thesis (Avatar → Voice → Pillars → Content). This is what we'd put in the Build Your Brand operating manual.

MOVE 01

Locate the slot you'll own

Dunford + Ries/Trout. Don't be a better X. Be the first credible Y. For Sunny: "the embodied AI marketing coach for men's coaches."

Feeds: Brand OS · Avatar step
MOVE 02

Write a real voice for 12 months

One channel. Daily-ish. POV-led. Voice rituals (Wispr → polish). The "wrong year" of writing is the cost of distinct.

Feeds: Brand OS · Voice step
MOVE 03

Build 3–5 pillars and never drift

Embodied marketing, AI leverage, men's-work integration, business design, breath/practice. Every post slots into one. Repetition without monotony.

Feeds: Brand OS · Pillars step
MOVE 04

Run the 60/40 split

Binet & Field. 60% of effort = brand (Signal, blog, podcast, POV). 40% = activation (quiz funnel, $7 trip-wire, $2K cohort, $12K offer).

Feeds: Build Your Brand · funnel
MOVE 05

Make the offer ladder unmistakable

Free Brand OS → $7 trip-wire → $2K cohort → $12K Build Your Brand. Each rung has one job. Hormozi math at every step, embodied tone all the way through.

Feeds: Brand OS Agent · funnel · cohort
MOVE 06

Automate the scaffolding, never the soul

AI runs repurposing, research, scheduling, formatting, enrichment. You hold POV, presence, pricing, mission. Atlas + Loki + Mira keep the wheels turning. You stay in the body.

Feeds: Agent squad · Brand OS Agent
