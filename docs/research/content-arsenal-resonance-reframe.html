The one structural change
Books → Body

Right now every piece starts from a book — Cialdini, Hormozi, Schwartz — and you narrate it. That makes you a synthesizer, not a man who's done the work. The Prosperous Coach is blunt about this: you create clients from who you are, not from cleverness. Flip the source. Start each piece from a real moment in your body or your men's work — then, if it helps, name the book that confirms it. The canon becomes the footnote, you become the source. Same intelligence, opposite center of gravity.

The six shifts
1

Books → Body

Source from lived experience; cite the canon only as proof. You're the well, not the narrator. (Above.)

2

Persuasion-tech: gospel → foil

Cialdini/Hormozi/Schwartz teach the manipulation game your brand tells coaches to quit. Use them as the thing you push against — "here's what the industry does, here's why it's the wrong layer for work this real" — never as tactics to deploy.

3

Mode: inform → transmit

Most carousels explain a marketing concept (they nod). Your own Script 1 thesis: the content that converts makes them feel a shift (they move). Weight the arsenal toward the embodied reels, away from the explainer carousels.

4

Domain: marketing coach → embodied man

Your unfair advantage is the intersection — men's work, love & leadership, the nervous system. The arsenal aims at generic "$5K–$30K coaches." Lead from your real domain and let marketing be the application. Nobody else can stand where you stand.

5

Cadence: volume → depth

"Ship a quarter's content in a week / 25 pieces" is a push brag — the exact thing the brand disowns. Prosperous Coach: a few powerful pieces beat a flood. Ship 5 that are most you, deep. Let the other 20 wait.

6

CTA: scattered → BRAND / SCORE

"Link in bio," "free 5-day mini-course," "quiz/cohort/$12K/list" — too many doors. Two only: BRAND → Brand OS, SCORE → Resonance Score quiz. And serve first; the invitation comes quiet, last.
