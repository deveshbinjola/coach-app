Progressive Disclosure Onboarding

Show coaches the ONE surface tied to their stated pain. Reveal the rest as they need it, surfaced through a weekly check-in that feels like a coach asking, not software gating.

Version: v1.0
Date: 2026-05-13
Source: Lex feedback (108 days group)
Status: Ready for build
Owner: Sunny Binjola

The pattern in one sentence

The quiz already collects intent. Onboarding opens to one home surface tied to that intent. Everything else lives in a quieter "More" lane that stays visible. Every Monday, a one-question check-in asks what's blocking the coach this week. If the answer shifts, the home surface shifts with it.

Why this matters for the brand

Linear, Granola, Stripe gravity. Subtract harder than competitors add. The platform never feels like it is gating features. It feels like it is respecting what the coach told it they needed. That's the wedge against GHL and HubSpot, where every screen shouts every capability.

What "home surface" means in practice:

1. Post-login redirect goes straight to that surface, not a generic dashboard.

2. The hero card on the surface is the primary action ("Generate today's caption," "Add 5 new leads," "Map your voice").

3. The side nav opens with that surface expanded, sub-actions visible.

4. The other surfaces sit under a "More" header, collapsed, visible but quiet.

3. Weekly check-in

Every Monday morning (or a configurable day per coach), a single in-app card or email opens with one question. The check-in is the wedge. It does three things at once: it surfaces the next feature at the moment of need, it generates a usage signal you can reflect back to the coach over time, and it reads like a coach checking in rather than software requesting feedback.

What's blocking you this week?
Not enough leads
Stuck on content
Voice doesn't sound like me
Ops eating my time
Same as last week
Something else

Behavior on response

If the coach picks something different from their current home surface, the dashboard shifts: the new pain becomes the home surface, the old one moves to "More" with a soft tag "recently primary." If they pick "same as last week," nothing changes. If they pick "something else," capture as free text and route to support / Sunny review.

4. v1 surfacing rules (no ML)

Three simple rules. All run on login. Each is a few lines of code. No model training, no analytics dashboards.

RULE 1

Stale home surface

If the pinned surface has zero events in the last 14 days, soft prompt on next login: "How's the [voice / leads / content / ops] piece going? Want to focus somewhere else?"

last_used_at < NOW() - 14 days

RULE 2

Cross-surface signal

If they've used the home surface heavily (more than 5 actions in 7 days) but never touched the natural next-step surface, suggest it as a card on the dashboard.

Default pairs:

· Heavy Voice use, no Content activity → suggest Content Studio ("Your voice profile is ready. Want to publish with it?")

· Heavy Content use, no Lead Inbox activity → suggest Lead Inbox ("Your content is doing work. Let's manage what comes back.")

· Heavy Lead Inbox use, no Client Center activity → suggest Client Center ("You're booking calls. Let's prep them.")

RULE 3

No pestering

If a coach tries a feature once and doesn't return within 7 days, do nothing. The feature stays in "More." Don't notify, don't email, don't prompt. The weekly check-in is the only proactive surface change.

5. Data model additions

Three small tables added to the existing cp_* schema. Nothing fancy. Indexes on user_id.

-- 1. Stored coach intent and its history cp_user_intent ( user_id uuid PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES cp_users(id), primary_intent text NOT NULL, -- voice | leads | content | ops intent_set_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW(), intent_history jsonb DEFAULT '[]' -- [{intent, at}, ...] ) -- 2. Lightweight feature usage tracking cp_feature_usage ( user_id uuid REFERENCES cp_users(id), feature_key text NOT NULL, -- voice_map | content_draft | lead_add | client_view ... last_used_at timestamptz NOT NULL, total_uses int DEFAULT 1, PRIMARY KEY (user_id, feature_key) ) -- 3. Weekly check-in responses cp_check_in ( id bigserial PRIMARY KEY, user_id uuid REFERENCES cp_users(id), week_of date NOT NULL, response text, -- leads | content | voice | ops | same | other free_text text, -- if response = other intent_changed bool DEFAULT false, created_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW(), UNIQUE (user_id, week_of) )

6. What v1 is NOT

Scope discipline. Anything below is intentionally out of v1 even if it's tempting.

Not in v1

  • ML pattern detection or predictive feature suggestion
  • Usage analytics dashboards for the coach
  • A/B testing infrastructure
  • Proactive notifications outside the Monday check-in
  • Feature tours, tutorial overlays, or onboarding tooltips
  • Configurable check-in cadence (default Monday only, ship custom later)
  • Multi-intent / blended home surface (one surface only, by design)

7. Voice and copy guardrails

Rules for every line of copy in this flow

Sound like a coach, not a product. "What's blocking you this week?" beats "Help us improve your experience."

Never gate. "When you're ready for X, it's right here" beats "X unlocked!"

Zero em-dashes. Strip them from every string. They're an AI tell that undermines the "writes in your voice" positioning.

Singular focus framing. "Your focus this week is X" beats "Here's your dashboard."

Short, declarative lines. No marketing copy. No exclamation points. No emoji except the one star (★) marking the pinned surface.

8. Build sequence

Sprint 1 (data + routing): Add the three tables. Wire post-login redirect to read cp_user_intent.primary_intent and route to the matching surface. Update the side nav to render pinned surface + "More" lane.

Sprint 2 (check-in): Build the Monday check-in card and email. Wire the response to update cp_user_intent and shuffle the side nav. Ship without rules, just the manual mechanism.

Sprint 3 (rules): Add the three v1 rules running on login. Test with the existing 15–20 free-access cohort.

Sprint 4 (instrument and iterate): Pull weekly check-in responses into a simple report Sunny reviews. Look for pattern: which pains shift, which stay, where coaches drop off. Decide whether to invest in rule 4+ or hold at three.

9. Success metrics

Activation: % of new coaches who take the home-surface primary action within 24h of signup.

Check-in engagement: % of active coaches who respond to the Monday check-in. Target 60%+ for v1.

Cross-surface adoption: % of coaches who eventually use a second surface within 30 days. This is the test of the pattern: people should grow into the platform, not bounce off the first screen.

Founder signal (qualitative): when Sunny talks to a pilot coach, does the platform feel like a coaching relationship or a tool? That's the unmeasurable but real win.
