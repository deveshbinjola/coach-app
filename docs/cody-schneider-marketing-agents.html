What to steal · ranked

The moves worth your time

1

Stand up a spend→revenue table (skip ClickHouse)

You already run Supabase. One table joining ad spend → sessions → Stripe revenue gives you the warehouse read-layer without new infra. This is the spine that turns your brain from "strategy agent" into "marketing agent" by Cody's own test.

effort: ~1 day · unlocks everything downstream
2

Build the pain-scrape → angle-rank step first

Highest-leverage, lowest-risk. Scrape Reddit/forums for your ICP (men's embodiment coaches, and separately Prism's chiropractic/med-spa world), rank complaints by frequency. This feeds ad copy, blog angles, AND video scripts. Pure upside, no ad account needed to start.

effort: ~half day · reusable across every channel
3

Adopt "creative is targeting" as a content law

Even before you automate ads: every static/reel should embed the pain in visible text + script so the algorithm can self-target. Bake it into your carousel + men's-work IG engine now. Free lift on organic too.

effort: mindset shift · applies to work you're already doing
4

Wire Meta API writes behind approval gates — later

The elimination bracket (publish 10 → run 3 days → cut → scale) is the endgame, but it needs live ad spend and the warehouse first. Keep it approval-gated (your YC-2026 audit-log pattern). Prove it on your own $ before you sell it to Prism.

effort: multi-day · sequence AFTER 1 & 2
