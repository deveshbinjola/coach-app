The Wedge & the Ladder

An app with many features only pays off when people climb in. So we don't open all the doors at once — we open one easy door, then let each win reveal the next.

The wedge — the one easy thing

Smallest input, biggest "whoa." No setup. The thing a coach does on day one and keeps doing.
"Speak it.
It sounds like you."

Tap the mic, talk for 20 seconds about anything on your mind. Get back a post in your voice, ready to publish. No Brand OS, no forms, no blank page.

Why it wins: lowest friction, daily habit, instant artifact — and it's on-brand. Embodiment coaches speak from the body. Typing is the head.
