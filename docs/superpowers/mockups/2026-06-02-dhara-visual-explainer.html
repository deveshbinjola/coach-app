Memory on a leash #6

Auto-learning is the scary part — wrong facts could compound. So every memory climbs a confidence ladder, carries its origin, and you can forget anything.
Candidate

Heard once. Flavors understanding, never stated as fact. Shown softly, with where it came from.

→
Repeated

Came up again. Promoted automatically — now trusted enough to rely on.

→
Confirmed

You said "remember this," or confirmed it. Stated as known fact.

Every memory shows provenance ("learned from your Tue chat") and lives in a "What Dhara remembers" view where you edit or forget it. Soft-deleted, never gone — auditable.
