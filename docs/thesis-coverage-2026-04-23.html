Two things to add to the thesis

The thesis is tight. These are the gaps I'd name before shipping it to a sales call.

Problem 0

Identity gap — the upstream leak your thesis doesn't name

Your 8 problems assume the coach has a clear voice and audience. In practice, most don't — which is why Brand OS Agent exists. Name it: "Brand OS writes the content → Coach Platform makes it into pipeline." That's the product story the thesis is implicitly reaching for but never says out loud, and it's the only reason your positioning ("the OS that turns content into pipeline") is defensible. You own both ends.

Dollarize

Price anchor every leak, not just referral

Only P8 has a number attached ("1 referral covers annual subscription"). Every other leak needs a back-of-envelope cost or the sales call will stall. Example for P4: "20 unqualified calls/month × 45 min = 15 hours × $500/hr = $7,500/month burning." That turns a feature list into ROI math. Ship a one-pager with a dollar amount on each leak before your next pitch.
