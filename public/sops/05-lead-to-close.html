The Funnel
Four Stages to Close
Capture

Stage 1: Lead Capture

Lead enters via quiz, trip-wire, DM, or referral. Auto-created in cp_leads with source tracking. Trip-wire buyers get immediate personal DM from Sunny.

< 4 hrs for hot leads
Qualify

Stage 2: Qualification

Three-question filter: What do you coach? How do you get clients now? What would change if follow-up was automatic? Qualify on pain level + revenue.

> 40% qualify rate
Discover

Stage 3: Discovery Call

30-minute call: rapport → pain → cost of inaction → live demo with THEIR data → offer → objection handling → close or next step.

> 60% call booked > 30% close rate
Close

Stage 4: Close

Send Stripe link on-call if possible. If "not now" → 3-day follow-up DM, 7-day follow-up, then nurture. After 14 days with no response, stop active pursuit.

< 14 days lead to paid
The Filter
Qualify or Disqualify

Qualify — book the call

  • Active coach with clients
  • Manual lead follow-up (spreadsheets, DMs, memory)
  • Frustrated with consistency or volume
  • Revenue $5K+/mo (can afford, will see ROI)

Disqualify — nurture instead

  • Not a coach or creator
  • Revenue under $3K/mo
  • Locked into competing tool (annual contract)
  • "Just looking" energy, no specific pain
The Call
Discovery Call Structure (30 min)
0–5

Rapport

"What's your coaching business look like right now?"

5–10

Pain

"Walk me through what happens when a new lead comes in"

10–15

Cost of Inaction

"How many leads slipped through the cracks last month?"

15–22

Live Demo

Show THEIR data in the platform. Draft a message live in their voice. This is the moment.

22–25

The Offer

Present the right tier. Price. What's included. Founding pricing if available.

25–30

Close or Next Step

"Want to get started today?" or "When do you want to decide by?"

Objections
What They'll Say & How to Respond
"It's too expensive"
It's $100/mo. If it books you one extra client this quarter, what's that worth? Most coaches tell me one client = $2K-$10K.
"I need to think about it"
Totally fair. What specifically do you need to think through? I might be able to answer it now.
"I already have a CRM"
Does your CRM draft messages in your voice? The CRM is the container — the AI voice is the unlock.
"I don't have enough leads"
That's actually the best time to set this up. When leads start coming, the system is already running.
"What if the AI doesn't sound like me?"
Let me show you. Give me 3 sentences you'd write to a lead. I'll run it through the voice engine right now.
