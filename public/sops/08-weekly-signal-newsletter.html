Generation

Draft Generation Process

Source Material

  1. Past 7 days of blog posts from /blog/
  2. Intel reports from /intel/
  3. External AI/coaching industry news
  4. Any notable platform updates or features shipped

Hard Rules

  • No em-dashes
  • No invented statistics — every number must have a source
  • Word target: 380-450
  • If fewer than 4 blog posts exist from the past 7 days, flag it and either:
    • Pull from past 14 days, or
    • Propose skipping this week (rare — avoid)

Quality

Quality Checklist (Sunny reviews before sending)

  • 3-2-1 structure followed (3 tech, 2 ideas, 1 tool)
  • Within 380-450 words
  • No em-dashes
  • No made-up statistics
  • Links work (spot-check 2-3)
  • CTA present (usually: reply to this email, or link to quiz/platform)
  • Subject line is specific, not generic ("3 AI tools that replace your VA" not "This week's Signal")
  • Preview text is different from subject line
  • Sent from correct sender (The Signal / ElevateAI)
