Owner: Sunny (decisions), Atlas (tracking)
Trigger: Coach suggests a feature or asks "can it do X?"
Goal: Every request captured, prioritized, and communicated back

Step 1

Capture

Every feature request gets logged in Notion (Feature Requests board) with:

FieldValue
TitleShort description of the request
RequesterCoach name + email
ChannelWhere they asked (Slack, email, call)

PriorityP1 (blocking) / P2 (painful) / P3 (nice-to-have)
Status
Captured → Under review → Planned → Building → Shipped → Won't do
Step 2

Acknowledge

Reply within 24 hours. Never leave a request without a response.

Template — request received:

Love this idea. Writing it down now. Can I ask -- what's the
specific situation where you'd use this? Helps me prioritize.

Template — already planned:

Great minds -- this is already on the roadmap for [timeframe].
I'll ping you when it's live so you can try it first.

Template — won't do (be honest):

I hear you on this. Thought about it and here's my honest take:
[reason it doesn't fit -- too niche, conflicts with simplicity,
better solved another way]. What if instead we [alternative]?
Step 3

Prioritize

Weekly review (Sunny, 30 min, Fridays)

Pull all new requests from the week and score:

FactorWeightQuestion
Frequency3xHow many coaches asked for this?
Pain3xIs this blocking revenue or causing churn?
Effort2xHow long to build? (hours, days, weeks)
Alignment2xDoes this fit the product vision?

Scoring:

  • 3+ coaches asked + blocking pain + < 1 day effort → Build this week
  • 2+ coaches asked + painful + < 1 week effort → Plan for next sprint
  • 1 coach asked + nice-to-have → Backlog (revisit if more requests come in)
  • Conflicts with product vision → Won't do (communicate why)
Step 4

Communicate

When you start building it:

Hey [name] -- remember when you asked about [feature]? Building
it this week. I'll let you know when it's live.

When you ship it:

DM the requester + mention in Founder's Friday:

[Name] -- [feature] is live. You asked for it, we built it.
Try it out and let me know how it feels.

Founder's Friday roadmap section:

Every Friday email includes:

  • 1 thing shipped this week
  • 1 thing being built next week

This keeps coaches feeling like the product is alive and responsive.

Anti-Patterns

What NOT to Do

  • Don't promise timelines you can't keep. "Soon" is better than "next week" if you're not sure.
  • Don't build for one coach unless the feature benefits everyone. One person's edge case is not a priority.
  • Don't say "great idea!" to everything. Be honest about what fits and what doesn't.
  • Don't go dark on requests. Even "this is in the backlog" is better than silence.
