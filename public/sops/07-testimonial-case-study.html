Deep Dive

The 5-Question Case Study Framework

For deeper case studies (quarterly), use this structure:

  1. Before: "What was your coaching business like before? How were you handling leads and content?"
  2. Pain: "What was the biggest frustration or bottleneck?"
  3. Decision: "What made you try ElevateAI / join the cohort?"
  4. Process: "Walk me through what the first few weeks looked like."
  5. Result: "What's different now? Any specific numbers — clients booked, time saved, revenue impact?"
Always ask for specifics:
  • "How many hours per week did you spend on follow-up before vs now?"
  • "How many discovery calls did you book in the last 30 days?"
  • "What's the revenue impact — even a rough estimate?"

Compliance

Permission & Compliance

Always get explicit permission before using anyone's words or likeness.

  • Text testimonial: "Can I use this quote with your name on our website and social?"
  • Video: "I'll send you the edit before it goes anywhere. You approve or kill it."
  • Screenshot: "Mind if I screenshot this and share it? I'll tag you."

Store permission type in the testimonial file (verbal, written, screenshot).

If they say no: Thank them, move on. Never push. Never use without permission.
