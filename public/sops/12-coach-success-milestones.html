Automation

Detection & Automation

Currently manual (Atlas checks weekly):

-- Coaches who hit 10 drafts this week
select c.full_name, c.email, count(m.id) as drafts
from cp_coaches c
join cp_lead_messages m on m.coach_id = c.id
where m.created_at > now() - interval '7 days'
group by c.id, c.full_name, c.email
having count(m.id) >= 10;

-- Coaches with 30+ days since signup who logged in 15+ times
-- (requires login tracking -- not built yet)

-- Leads that moved to 'client' status this week
select l.full_name, l.email, l.coach_id, l.updated_at
from cp_leads l
where l.status = 'client'
and l.updated_at > now() - interval '7 days';

Guardrails

Anti-Patterns

  • Don't celebrate too often. If someone gets a notification every day, they'll tune out. Max 1 celebration per coach per week.
  • Don't celebrate vanity metrics. "You logged in 5 days in a row!" feels hollow. Celebrate outcomes (drafts sent, clients converted).
  • Don't make it feel automated even when it is. Personal touch matters. Use their name, reference something specific.
  • Don't ask for testimonial on EVERY milestone. Ask once after a Tier 2+ milestone. If they say no, wait 60 days before asking again.
