Step 1

Acknowledge

Reply to the coach immediately with:

Got it -- looking into this now. I'll update you within [timeframe based on severity].
Never say:
  • "It works on my end" (useless)
  • "Can you try again?" (without investigating first)
  • "That's a known issue" (unless you can also say when it's fixed)
Step 2

Reproduce

1. Get the details:

  • What were they doing? (exact page, exact action)
  • What did they expect to happen?
  • What actually happened? (screenshot if possible)
  • What browser/device?
  • What email are they logged in with? (to check their data)

2. Check the logs:

  • Cloudflare Pages deployment logs
  • Supabase logs (get_logs via MCP)
  • Browser console errors (ask coach for screenshot if needed)

3. Reproduce locally:

  • Log in as that coach (use admin client to check their data)
  • Follow their exact steps
  • If can't reproduce: ask for screen recording
Step 3

Fix

  1. Create a branch: fix/[short-description]
  2. Write the fix — minimal, targeted, no scope creep
  3. Test locally — reproduce the bug, verify fix, check for regressions
  4. Build check: npx next build must pass clean
  5. Create PR with:
    • What broke
    • Why it broke
    • What the fix does
    • How to verify
Step 4

Deploy

  1. Merge to main — Cloudflare Pages auto-deploys
  2. Wait 2-3 minutes for deploy to propagate
  3. Verify in production — hit the exact flow that was broken
  4. Notify the coach:
Fixed! [Brief description of what happened]. Try it now --
should be working. Let me know if anything else feels off.
Step 5

Post-mortem (P0 and P1 only)

Within 24 hours of resolution, write a short note:

[2-3 sentences]

[1-2 sentences]

[What was changed]
