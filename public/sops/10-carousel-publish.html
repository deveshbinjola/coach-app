Brief

Brief Requirements

Every carousel needs:

  1. Framework name — the teaching concept (e.g., T.A.S.T.E., 4-Worker Stack)
  2. Core insight — the one thing the reader walks away knowing
  3. CTA keyword — what they comment to get the lead magnet (e.g., "TASTE", "STACK")
  4. Visual for slide 5 — does the framework have a visual? (chart, grid, meter, etc.)

Quality Gate

Hook Quality Gate

Before building the carousel, the hook must pass:

  • Max ~30 words, 4-6 lines at 135px
  • Identity-level — names WHO the reader IS
  • Felt before understood — reader feels something first
  • Last word(s) carry the contradiction (green accent)
  • No framework names or jargon on slide 1
  • Slide 2 deepens the loop, doesn't just continue
Cliche diagnostic:
  • Abstract noun + grandiose verb? Kill it.
  • Could any LinkedIn coach have written this? Rewrite.
  • Longer than 6 lines? Too long.
Design

Brand Design System

  • Dark bg: #0A0F1C / Light bg: #F0EDE6
  • Accent (dark): #00FF41 / Accent (light): #0B6E23
  • Error/pain: rgba(255,60,60,0.7)
  • Hook font: Plus Jakarta Sans 800, 135px, ALL CAPS
  • Headlines: Playfair Display 900, 64-96px
  • Body: Plus Jakarta Sans 400-600, 28-36px
  • Signature: Caveat 700, 36px, "by Sunny"

Review

Cassie Review Checklist

Cassie checks for brand alignment before Sunny approves:

  • Colors match brand system (no off-brand tones)
  • Typography hierarchy correct (hook → headline → body)
  • Slide 5 visual is clear and readable
  • Green accents used on key words, not overused
  • "by Sunny" signature on slide 1
  • Counter + "ELEVATE AI" watermark on slides 2-10
  • No orphaned words or awkward line breaks
  • CTA slide is clear: "Comment [KEYWORD]"
Publishing

Publishing Checklist

  • All 10 slides exported and verified
  • Caption written (Loki drafts, Sunny edits)
  • Hashtags selected (5-10, mix of niche + broad)
  • CTA keyword set up in DM automation (if using ManyChat or similar)
  • Posted at optimal time (check IG insights for best time)
  • First comment posted immediately (hook continuation or CTA repeat)
