Phases
What Happens Each Week
Week -4 to -2

Seed

  • Post 2-3 content pieces/week seeding the cohort topic
  • Add CTA: "DM me WAITLIST for first access"
  • Target: 30+ people on waitlist before announcement
  • Sales page staged (not published)
  • Stripe product + price created
Week -2

Warm

  • DM past cohort grads for referrals
  • DM waitlist: "You're on my short list. Want details?"
  • DM warm IG/LinkedIn contacts who fit ICP
  • Goal: 5-6 soft yeses before public announcement
Week -1

Announce

  • IG carousel + LinkedIn post + email to full list
  • "10 spots. 8 weeks. $2,000. Here's what you get."
  • Include who it's for AND who it's NOT for
  • Cal.com discovery call link for fence-sitters
Week 0

Cart Open (7 days)

  • 5-email sequence fires (see below)
  • Discovery calls for fence-sitters
  • DM nudges on Day 0 + Day 6
  • Update seat count in every email (honest numbers)
Week +1

Close & Welcome

  • "Sold out" post (even if 7 of 10 — "cohort is full")
  • Welcome email + Slack invite + intake form
  • Add members to offerings + Cal.com group event
  • Notify waitlist who didn't join about next cohort
Week +2

Kickoff

  • Cohort Week 1: intros + Brand OS overview
  • Homework: 5-min voice memo about ideal client
  • Weekly calls begin on set day/time for 8 weeks
Cart Open Week
5-Email Sales Sequence
Day 0
"Doors are open — 10 spots"
Full announcement email. What you get, who it's for, founding pricing, payment link.
→ Payment link
Day 2
"[X] spots claimed — here's what [grad name] said"
Social proof. Testimonial from past cohort grad. Updated seat count.
→ Payment link
Day 4
"Is the Augmented Coach Cohort right for you?"
Objection buster. Answers the 3 most common questions: niche fit, technical ability, price.
→ Cal.com call link
Day 6
"[X] spots left — closing tomorrow at midnight"
Urgency. Seat count. Cart closes tomorrow. "Reply now — I'll answer tonight."
→ Payment link
Day 7
"Closing in 6 hours" (send at 6 PM PT)
Final call. Short. Direct. Payment link. "See you inside."
→ Payment link (last chance)
Prep
Pre-Launch Checklist

Complete before Week -1

Sales page written and staged (not published)
Stripe product and price created ($2,000 one-time or payment plan)
5-email sequence written and loaded in Resend/Beehiiv
Cal.com cohort discovery call slot configured
Cohort Slack channel created (private, invite-only)
Intake form ready (Notion/Typeform)
DM templates prepped for warm outreach
Past cohort grads contacted for referral + testimonial
Waitlist has 30+ names
After
Post-Cohort (Week 9+)

Graduation

  • Send graduation email + certificate
  • Ask for testimonial (see Testimonial SOP)
  • Offer platform founding/standard pricing

Alumni Network

  • Add to alumni Slack channel
  • Invite to quarterly alumni calls
  • First to hear about new features + next cohort

Referral Engine

  • Ask for 2-3 referrals for next cohort
  • Offer incentive (1 month free platform, etc.)
  • Tag referrers in cp_leads
