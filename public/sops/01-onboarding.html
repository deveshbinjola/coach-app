Standard Operating Procedure

New Coach Onboarding

From payment to first AI-drafted message in under 24 hours

Activation target: Coach sends their first AI-drafted message within 24 hours of signup
Phase 1
Instant — Stripe Webhook Fires

Payment recorded

Stripe checkout.session.completed → cp_payments row created with amount, session ID, customer email

Lead created or upgraded

Existing lead → status set to "client". New email → lead created with source "Stripe payment"

Client room + offering membership

Room created with payment_status: paid. Offering membership linked with role: member, status: active

Welcome email sent

Resend fires: "You're in. Here's your next 24 hours." Contains Cal.com kickoff link, login link, Slack invite

Phase 2 — First 24 Hours
White-Glove Kickoff Call

Pre-call prep (15 min)

Check cp_coaches — do they have a voice profile?
Check cp_leads — did they import anything?
Pull up their Brand OS output (if quiz funnel)
Have their email open in the platform for screenshare
0–5 min
Rapport
How's business going? What's the biggest bottleneck right now?
5–20 min
Voice Profile Setup
Walk through the 5-question VoiceSetupFlow together. Build their voice live.
20–30 min
Lead Import
Paste in their top 10 active leads — name, email, one line of context each.
30–45 min
First AI Draft
Pick a lead, click draft, read together, adjust tone. This is the magic moment.
45–55 min
Platform Tour
Inbox, status pipeline, follow-up reminders, content desk overview.
55–60 min
Set Expectations
"By Friday, send 10 AI-drafted messages. Reply to my check-in."

Post-call DM (within 1 hour)

"Great call. Your voice profile is live. Try drafting a message to [lead name] — the AI already knows your voice."

Tag in cp_leads

Add tags: founding-10, kickoff-complete. Note any feature requests in Notion Action Board.

Phases 3–5
The First Week
Day +1
"Your First Week" Guide
Auto-email: 5 numbered steps — add leads, try draft, set follow-ups, send 10 messages, reply to Friday check-in.
Automated
Day +3
Personal Check-in
Sunny personally messages in Slack: "How's it going? What's the friction so far?" Never automate this for founders.
Sunny — manual
Day +7
Founder's Friday
Weekly email: 1 win to celebrate, 1 thing shipped, 1 thing building, 1 ask from the group.
Sunny — weekly
Activation
Coach Is "Activated" When

All four must be true

Voice profile created — cp_voice_profiles.active = true
At least 1 lead imported — cp_leads count > 0
Audience questions answered — audience_self + audience_serves set
At least 1 AI draft sent — tracked in cp_lead_messages
Escalation
When Things Go Wrong
No login within 48 hours
Sunny sends personal DM + offers a call
No AI draft sent within 7 days
Sunny calls directly — something is wrong
Coach says "this isn't working"
30-min troubleshoot call within 24 hours
Coach asks for refund (within 30 days)
Honor it, no questions. Ask for 2 min of feedback.
