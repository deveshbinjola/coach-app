Templates

Email Templates

Email 1 — Day 0: Payment Failed

Subject: Payment failed — quick fix
[First name] --

Your monthly payment of $[amount] didn't go through.
Usually it's an expired card or a bank hold.

Update your payment method here:
-> [Stripe customer portal link]

Stripe will auto-retry in 3 days, but updating now means no
interruption.

Sunny

Email 2 — Day 3: Second Failure

Subject: Second try didn't work either
[First name] --

Payment still failing after the second attempt.
Stripe will try once more on [date].

Takes 30 seconds to fix:
-> [Stripe customer portal link]

After the third attempt, your account will pause (your data
stays safe -- nothing gets deleted).

If something's going on financially, reply -- happy to work
something out.

Sunny

Email 3 — Day 8: Account Paused

Subject: Account paused — your data is safe
[First name] --

After 3 failed payment attempts, your account is now in
read-only mode. You can still log in and see your data,
but AI drafting and new lead imports are paused.

Nothing gets deleted. Your leads, voice profile, and drafts
are all safe.

To reactivate instantly:
-> [Stripe customer portal link]

If you'd rather cancel, reply "cancel" and I'll process it
with no questions.

Sunny

Day 10: Sunny Personal DM

Channel: Slack (founders) or IG/email (standard)

Hey [name] -- saw your payment bounced a few times. Just wanted
to check in personally. Is everything okay? If it's a cash flow
thing, I'm happy to work out a different arrangement. No judgment.

Email 4 — Day 21: Last Chance

Subject: Last chance before we close your account
[First name] --

Your account has been paused for 3 weeks. Your data is still
safe, but I'll need to close the account on [date -- Day 30]
if the payment issue isn't resolved.

Three options:

1. Update your card -> [Stripe portal link] (reactivates instantly)
2. Reply "pause" -> I'll hold your data for 60 more days, no charge
3. Reply "cancel" -> I'll close the account and you'll get a data
   export within 24 hours

Sunny

Configuration

Stripe Configuration

  • Smart retries: Enabled (Stripe's ML picks optimal retry times)
  • Retry schedule: 3 attempts over 8 days
  • Failed payment webhook: invoice.payment_failed → fires dunning emails
  • Subscription cancelled webhook: customer.subscription.deleted → triggers churn SOP
  • Customer portal: Enable self-serve card update at billing.stripe.com/p/[config]

Edge Cases

Edge Cases

Coach says "I can't afford it right now"

  • Offer to pause for 60 days at no charge (hold their data)
  • Or offer a reduced rate for 2 months ($50/mo instead of $100)
  • Document the arrangement in Notion

Coach says "I didn't know I was being charged"

  • This shouldn't happen if onboarding is good
  • Refund the last charge, no questions
  • Figure out what went wrong in the signup flow

Founding member payment fails

  • Always escalate to Sunny personal outreach on Day 0 (not Day 10)
  • Founding members get more patience — they're co-building with you
