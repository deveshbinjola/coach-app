Goal: Understand why, save if possible, part well if not

Voluntary Flow

Voluntary Cancellation Flow

Step 1: Cancellation Request Received

If they click cancel in-app (future — not built yet):

  • Show a 1-screen exit survey before processing:
    • "What's the main reason?" (pick one)
      • Not using it enough
      • Too expensive for what I get
      • Missing a feature I need
      • Switching to another tool
      • Business is pausing/closing
      • Other
    • "Anything we could have done differently?" (optional text)
  • Store response in cp_churn_feedback (create this table when building the feature)

If they email/DM to cancel:

  • Acknowledge within 4 hours
  • Ask: "Before I process this — mind sharing what's not working? 2 sentences is enough."
  • Process cancellation regardless of whether they respond (never hold someone hostage)

Step 2: Process the Cancellation

  1. Cancel subscription in Stripe (end of billing period, not immediate)
  2. Update cp_coaches.plan to cancelled (or create a status field)
  3. Send confirmation email:
Subject: Your account is cancelled
[First name] --

Done. Your access runs through [billing period end date].

Your data stays intact for 90 days. If you come back within that
window, everything will be right where you left it.

If you ever want to restart: app.elevateaisystem.com -- same login.

No hard feelings. I hope the work we did on your voice and leads
was useful regardless.

Sunny

Step 3: Win-back Sequence

Day 14 after cancellation:

Subject: Quick question, [first name]
[First name] --

It's been 2 weeks since you cancelled. No pitch -- just curious:

Did you find something that works better?
Or did the timing just not line up?

Either way, I'd love the honest answer. Helps me build a better
product for the coaches who stay.

Sunny

Day 45 after cancellation (only if they had > 5 leads in the system):

Subject: Your leads are still here
[First name] --

Your [X] leads and voice profile are still saved in the platform.
They'll be deleted at the 90-day mark ([date]).

If you want to pick back up -- same login, founding pricing still
honored if you were a founder.

No pressure. Just didn't want your data to disappear without a
heads-up.

Sunny

Day 85 — Final notice before data deletion:

Subject: Data deletion in 5 days
[First name] -- your ElevateAI data (leads, voice profile, drafts)
will be permanently deleted on [date].

Want to export it? Reply and I'll send you a CSV within 24 hours.
Want to reactivate? Just log in at app.elevateaisystem.com.

Sunny
Involuntary

Involuntary Churn (Dunning)

Stripe Payment Failure Sequence

Attempt 1 fails (Day 0):

  • Stripe auto-retries in 3 days
  • Send email immediately:
Subject: Payment failed — quick fix
[First name] --

Your monthly payment of $[amount] didn't go through.
Usually it's an expired card.

Update your payment method here: [Stripe customer portal link]

If you do nothing, Stripe will retry in 3 days.

Sunny

Attempt 2 fails (Day 3):

  • Stripe retries in 5 more days
  • Send email:
Subject: Second try didn't work either
[First name] -- payment still failing. Stripe will try one more
time on [date]. After that your account pauses.

Takes 30 seconds to fix: [Stripe portal link]

If something's going on financially, reply -- we'll figure it out.

Attempt 3 fails (Day 8):

  • Pause account access (read-only mode — they can see data but not use AI features)
  • Send email:
Subject: Account paused — your data is safe
[First name] -- after 3 failed attempts, your account is now paused.

Your leads, voice profile, and drafts are all safe. Nothing deleted.

To reactivate: update your card at [Stripe portal link] and you're
back instantly.

If you'd rather cancel, reply "cancel" and I'll process it.

Sunny

Day 30 of paused state with no action:

  • Treat as voluntary cancellation
  • Enter win-back sequence above
Prevention

Silent Churn Prevention

Weekly automated check (Atlas runs this):

Query coaches who:

  • Have payment_status = paid
  • Last login > 14 days ago
  • Have > 0 leads

Day 14 of inactivity — nudge:

Subject: Your leads are waiting
[First name] -- you have [X] leads in your inbox and haven't
logged in for 2 weeks.

[Lead name] last messaged [X days] ago. Want me to draft a
follow-up in your voice? Just reply "yes" and I'll send you
the draft.

Sunny

Day 21 of inactivity — personal outreach:

Sunny sends a personal DM (Slack for founders, email for standard):

Hey [name] -- haven't seen you in the platform for a few weeks.
Everything good? Want to hop on a quick call to troubleshoot?

Data Retention Policy

EventData kept forThen
Active subscriptionIndefinite—
Cancelled90 daysPermanent deletion
Payment paused60 days paused + 90 days after formal cancelPermanent deletion
Account never activated180 daysPermanent deletion

What gets deleted:

  • cp_leads rows scoped to that coach
  • cp_voice_profiles scoped to that coach
  • cp_lead_messages scoped to that coach
  • cp_client_rooms scoped to that coach
  • cp_coaches row itself

What gets kept (anonymized):

  • Payment records in cp_payments (for accounting/tax)
  • Aggregate usage stats (for product analytics)
