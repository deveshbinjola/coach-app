# Step 6 — Founding 10 Onboarding Sequence

The Augmented Coach Cohort is the natural launch list for the platform. They've already paid for community ($2K/8wk), they trust the brand, and they're actively trying to grow — exactly the wedge.

## Goal

10 founding coaches paying **$497 onboarding + $100/mo** within 14 days of launch.

## Why "founding"

- Locks in lifetime founder pricing ($100/mo vs. eventual $147–$197)
- Direct line to Sunny for product feedback
- Co-creation = case studies + testimonials = launch fuel for cohort 2
- Reframes the ask from "buy software" → "co-build the thing with me"

### Day +1 — "Your First Week" Guide

Plain markdown attached or linked HTML. 5 numbered steps:
1. Add your 5 hottest leads (5 min)
2. Click ✨ AI Draft on one — read what it produces — adjust
3. Set follow-ups for the next 3 days
4. By end of week, send 10 AI-drafted messages
5. Reply to the Friday check-in email with: 1 win + 1 bug

### Day +3 — Check-in (manual, NOT automated)

Sunny personally messages each founding member in Slack:
> "How's it going? What's the friction so far?"

**Why manual:** founding-stage feedback is gold. Automating this kills the relationship.

### Day +7 — Founder's Friday

Weekly Friday email to all founding 10:
- 1 win to celebrate (a coach booked a call from an AI draft, etc.)
- 1 thing shipped this week
- 1 thing being shipped next week
- 1 ask (what's broken? what's missing?)

## Tactical: how to actually send the sequence

### Easiest path (today)

1. Build the email list in Resend (~5 min)
2. Use Resend's broadcast feature OR Beehiiv segments for cohort grads
3. Send manually on Day 0 / 2 / 4 / 7
4. DM teaser on Day -3 done by hand from Sunny's IG/LinkedIn

### Better path (week 2)

1. Wire the Stripe `checkout.session.completed` webhook → Resend transactional
2. Welcome email auto-fires
3. "Day +1 First Week Guide" sends via Resend Audience automation
4. Founder's Friday becomes a Beehiiv segment

### Production path (month 2)

1. Move all transactional to Resend (bilingual templating)
2. Lifecycle marketing in Beehiiv (segments by `status` from cp_subscriptions)
3. Dunning sequences for `past_due` subscriptions
4. Re-engagement for `inactive_30d` (from `cp_lead_messages` activity)

## Tracking

Tag every founding 10 lead in `cp_leads` with `tags: ['founding-10', 'augmented-cohort-grad']`. That way you can pull a list anytime:

```sql
select full_name, email, status, last_contact_at
from cp_leads
where 'founding-10' = any(tags)
order by status;
```

Cross-reference `cp_subscriptions` to see who paid:

```sql
select c.full_name, c.email, s.status as subscription_status, s.onboarding_paid
from cp_coaches c
left join cp_subscriptions s on s.coach_id = c.id
order by s.created_at desc nulls last;
```
