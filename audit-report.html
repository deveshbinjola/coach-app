What's Working

The Good Stuff

These are genuine competitive advantages. Don't touch them.

Login Page — Best-in-Class

The split-screen with Generic AI vs Your Voice phone mockup is killer positioning. "Your next client is already in your DMs" is a great hook. The copy sells the product before they even sign in.

Command Center — Opinionated Dashboard

"Hey, Sunny. Take a breath before you start." + the Coach OS status bar + Insight Queue saying "What to do, what to skip" — this isn't a generic dashboard, it's a coach's cockpit. The 4 action cards give clear next steps, not just metrics.

Microcopy Throughout

"Keep the relationship moving after the sale." "Both written by AI. Only one sounds like a coach." Every page has copy that sounds human, opinionated, and coach-native. This is a rare strength most SaaS products never achieve.

Voice Page — Unique Concept

Voice asset status (66% locked, compounding), Brand OS completion, framework generator — nobody else has this. The "voice trust" concept of tracking how often you ship without editing is genuinely novel IP.

Content Pillars with "vs" Positioning

"Why Heartbreak Feels Crazy vs The 'just move on' industrial complex" — each pillar has a contrarian angle built in. This makes every content draft start with tension, not just a topic.

Lead Rescue Concept

Not just a lead list — it surfaces the 5 warmest conversations that need action NOW with a time estimate (20 min). This is how a chief of staff would brief you, not how a CRM would.

Settings as Progressive Disclosure

Numbered sections (Identity & Brand, Delivery, Advanced) with SET UP / DONE status badges. Feels like onboarding that lives permanently — you always know what's configured and what's not.

Brand Consistency

Green + navy + cream is consistent across every page. Typography is clean. Cards have consistent padding and border radius. No visual jarring anywhere. The product looks like one person designed it, not five.

Issues Found

What Needs Fixing

Ranked by impact. Critical = losing users. High = losing trust. Medium = losing polish.

1
Leads / Inbox

No search or filter on 329 leads

You have 329 active leads and zero way to search by name, email, tag, or temperature. A coach looking for "that guy from the breathwork event" has to scroll through the entire list. This is the #1 daily-use blocker.

Fix: Add a search bar + filter pills (Warm/Hot/Cold, source, tag) at the top of the leads list.
Critical
2
Leads / Inbox

No pagination — 329 leads in one scroll

The entire lead list loads as one flat scroll. Below the Lead Rescue section, it becomes a wall of identical-looking rows with no visual hierarchy. Every row has the same structure: name, status, "Draft next message", "None", "Edit".

Fix: Add pagination (25/page) or virtual scroll. Give the table rows avatars, color-coded temperature dots, and last-activity timestamps.
Critical
3
Mobile

No bottom tab bar on mobile

On mobile, the only navigation is the hamburger menu. Coaches check this app between sessions on their phone — needing two taps (hamburger + nav item) to switch sections kills momentum. Every competitor has a bottom tab bar.

Fix: Add a fixed bottom tab bar with 5 icons: Home, Leads, Clients, Voice, Content. Hide hamburger on mobile.
High
4
Clients

Duplicated client description

The client bio appears twice — once as static text below the name and again in the editable "Current focus" textarea with identical content. Confusing: which one is the source of truth? Why show it twice?

Fix: Remove the static text above "Current focus". The editable textarea IS the description.
High
5
Clients

Massive dead whitespace below client sidebar

The left sidebar shows 2 clients, then the entire lower-left quadrant of the page is completely empty. With Session Memory showing "No session notes yet", more than 50% of the visible page is blank. Feels like a beta product.

Fix: Stack Session Memory, Calendar, Homework, and Resources into a tighter column layout. Add a "Quick actions" strip or client timeline to fill dead space.
High
6
Clients → Offerings

Form always visible, empty state buried

The "New offering" form is always expanded at the top of the Offerings tab, even when there are zero offerings. The empty state message ("No offerings yet") is pushed below the fold. New users see a form before understanding what offerings are.

Fix: Show the empty state first with a prominent "+ Create your first offering" button. Move the form behind a button or modal.
High
7
404 Page

Missing nav bar + incomplete navigation cards

The 404 page has no navigation bar at all. The recovery cards only show 3 options (Home, Leads, Voice) — missing Clients and Content. If /leads is 404 on production, users hit a dead end with no way to navigate properly.

Fix: Add the standard nav bar to the 404 page. Include all 5 sections in recovery cards.
Medium
8
Command Center

Revenue + Weekly Reach cards are wasted space

Bottom of dashboard: Revenue card just says "Add your offerings" and Weekly Reach shows 0/30 with a tiny chart. These are prime real-estate taking up space with no actionable value until configured. On first visit, 30% of the dashboard is empty states.

Fix: Collapse empty-state cards into a single "Set up" checklist strip. Only expand Revenue/Reach cards once data exists.
Medium
9
Command Center

Lead rescue horizontal strip has no scroll indicator

The rescue names (Sarah Hennessy, Amy Jamieson, Ken Holdway) sit in a horizontally scrollable strip, but there's no visual cue that more names exist off-screen. Users may think there are only 3 when there are 5.

Fix: Add a fade/gradient on the right edge or show "2 more" indicator. Or stack them vertically on the lead rescue section.
Medium
10
Content

Single long scroll — no section navigation

Content page has: Editorial masthead, Pillars, Ship This Week, Buyer Mirror, Draft Room, Signal Stack, Latest Draft — all in one vertical scroll. No tabs, no anchors, no collapsible sections. Finding the Draft Room requires scrolling past everything.

Fix: Add anchor tabs at the top ("Desk | Drafts | Library") or collapsible sections so users can jump to what they need.
Medium
11
Leads / Inbox

Lead table rows have no visual differentiation

Below Lead Rescue, the full lead list is a flat table where every row looks identical. No avatars, no color-coded left borders by temperature, no inline last-message preview. Scanning 329 rows is a wall of sameness.

Fix: Add colored left-border by lead temperature. Show initials/avatar. Add a one-line last message preview.
Low
12
Clients

No manual "Add client" button

Clients can only come from lead conversion. There's no way to manually add a client who came through a referral, an event, or an offline conversation. Edge case but important for coaches.

Fix: Add "+ Add client" button in the sidebar header.
Low
13
Voice

Voice Trust card is oversized for its content

"Send 3 more AI-drafted messages and we'll start tracking..." takes up a full card width with a lot of empty space. One sentence doesn't need that much real estate.

Fix: Make it an inline banner or slim card, not a full-width block.
Low
14
Settings

No way to navigate back — no breadcrumb

Settings page has no "Back to Home" link or breadcrumb. The only way out is the nav bar. Minor, but feels like a dead-end page.

Fix: Add a "Back to dashboard" link below the Settings header.
Low
80/20 — Do These 4 Things

The Moves That Matter

These 4 changes will fix 80% of the perceived jank. Everything else is polish.

Add Search + Filters to Leads

Search bar at the top of the lead list. Filter pills for temperature (Warm/Hot/Cold), source, and tags. Pagination at 25/page. This single change transforms the app from "demo" to "daily driver" for any coach with more than 50 leads.

Impact: Unblocks daily use · Est. 1-2 days

Fix the Clients Page Layout

Remove the duplicated description. Tighten the card layout so Session Memory, Calendar, Homework, and Resources stack without massive dead space. Add an "Add client" button. This page is where coaches spend the most time — it should feel tight and useful, not empty.

Impact: Retention / daily engagement · Est. 1 day

Add Mobile Bottom Tab Bar

Fixed bottom bar with 5 icons: Home, Leads, Clients, Voice, Content. Active state matches the green brand color. Coaches live on their phones between sessions — this is table stakes for any mobile-used SaaS.

Impact: Mobile retention · Est. 0.5 day

Fix Empty States Everywhere

Offerings form always open, Revenue card showing nothing, Voice Trust oversized, 404 missing nav — collapse all empty states into slim banners or checklists. Show the full card only when there's data. Empty states shouldn't take up more space than populated ones.

Impact: First impression / trust · Est. 1 day
Verdict

Bottom Line

The design language, brand consistency, and microcopy are genuinely excellent — better than most funded SaaS products. The Login page and Command Center are ship-ready. The Voice and Content pages have unique IP that no competitor offers.

The weak spots are all in daily-use ergonomics: searching leads, managing clients, navigating on mobile. These aren't design problems — they're the gap between a demo product and a tool someone uses 10 times a day.

The 4 changes in the 80/20 section would close that gap in about 4 days of focused work.
